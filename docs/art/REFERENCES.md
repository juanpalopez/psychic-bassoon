# Reference images

Mood and layout references for the art direction. They are never shipped in the game.

| `reference-wide-road.jpg` | An angled view of a winding, wide dirt road with towers on round stone bases beside the bends, goblin foes, a gate for the spawn, a Heartstone on a carved plinth, cliffs, river, torches and glowing crystals, and a HUD with lives, gold, wave counter, tower buttons with costs and an ability bar. | A mock-up supplied by the owner on 2026-10-10 (tool and prompt not recorded). Converted to JPEG at 1536×1024 to keep the repository small. Reference only: do not trace it or copy characters from it. |

**What we take from it**

- **Road:** wide and curved, with worn grassy edges. Drawn by the renderer as a ribbon over the unchanged 1-cell grid (see Phase 5 in `docs/PLAN.md`).
- **Plots:** round stone bases with a row of small amber lights for the tower level.
- **Look:** dusk light, amber torches along the road, cyan, violet and yellow crystal glows, dark moss and slate ground.
- **HUD:** lives, gold and wave counter top left, tower buttons with costs bottom left, abilities bottom right, pause top right. The HUD design itself is Claude Design's job (Phase 6).
