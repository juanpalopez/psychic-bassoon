# Build from a bar along the bottom row, not from a menu floating by the socket

## Context
The build menu opened next to the tapped socket, and on a busy board it covered the road, foes or other sockets ("the HUD's floating menu sometimes blocks the user's actions", owner, 2026-10-19). Three redesigns were drawn on the HUD canvas: A, a build bar; B, a side drawer; C, a radial ring.

## Decision
Option A (owner, 2026-10-20). While a free socket is selected, the bottom HUD row becomes the build bar: one 88 px row of tower tokens plus Close. Auto and Start wave step aside, and nothing floats over the world. A socket that the bar or the top row would hide is eased to the middle at the same zoom. CLAUDE.md, UI section.

## Alternatives considered
- B, side drawer: it covers a fifth of the screen on a phone.
- C, radial ring: it sits over the board around the socket, which is the problem being fixed.

## Consequences
- The placement code of the floating menu and its 40-seed test are gone.
- The Toolbox Tray (spells, GDD) will share the bottom row; it hides while the bar is open.
