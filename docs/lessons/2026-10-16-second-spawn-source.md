# Add a second spawn source that merges into the main road after wave 10

## Context
The owner wants more late-game pressure and asked for a second spawn source that only activates after wave 10. The 2026-10-12 decision had been one road and one spawn.

## Decision
A feeder road from the top or bottom edge joins the main road; from wave 11 each wave's groups alternate between the two sources; the Messenger Crow announces it at wave 10 and a greyed marker shows the new spawn from the start. Supersedes the one-road decision (detours, branching and drawbridges stay dropped).

## Alternatives considered
- A separate full road to the Heartstone: most variety but the largest board change, may not fit 16x11.
- A mirrored road: simple generator but predictable.
- Extra foes on the new road: much harder, needs a rebalance.

## Consequences
- The generator gets its own random stream, so the main road and waves 1 to 10 do not change.
- Guards must also hold second-route foes once they are on the main road.
- Goldens, the balance sweep and the GDD change; ticket #268.
